# Code of conduct

Memvine is built in the open, and everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or
anywhere else the project meets) is expected to keep it a place people want to come back to.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Say what you mean plainly; criticism of work is welcome, contempt for people is not.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory language or unwelcome attention, in public or private.
- Respect people's time and privacy: do not post someone else's personal details or private
  messages.

If someone breaks these, write to **hello@memvine.dev**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept
confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads or block anyone who does not
stop.

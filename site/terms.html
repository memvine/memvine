Draft of 10 November 2026. A lawyer has not reviewed this page yet. Memvine is in an invited trial, and this page says in plain words how the trial works.

The service

Memvine is a hosted memory service for coding agents. Your team's agent plugins send session conversation to Memvine. Memvine turns it into a small set of memories and pages, and serves them back into later sessions.

These terms cover the hosted service at memvine.dev, app.memvine.dev and api.memvine.dev. The open source code is covered by its own licence in the repository.

Who can use it

The service is open by invitation. You need an invite to create an organization. You must be allowed to agree to these terms for your team, and you are responsible for the people you invite.

Your content

What your team sends to Memvine, and the memories made from it, stay yours. You give Memvine permission to store and process that content only to run the service for you.

Do not send content you have no right to share. Do not send passwords, keys, or personal data that your team is not allowed to put in a third party service.

You can export your data, forget a memory or delete a project from the console at any time.

Acceptable use

  • Do not try to reach another team's data.
  • Do not attack, overload or probe the service. If you find a security problem, tell us at hello@memvine.dev.
  • Do not use the service to break the law.
  • Keep your keys secret, and revoke a key that may have leaked.

An early service

Memvine is a trial. It can be wrong: a memory may be out of date or mistaken, so treat memories as notes from a teammate, not as fact. Check anything that matters.

The service may be unavailable at times and features may change. There is no uptime promise during the trial.

The service is provided as is, without warranties, as far as the law allows. As far as the law allows, Memvine is not liable for indirect losses or for lost data, and its total liability is limited to what you paid for the service in the last 12 months.

Price

The trial is free. If that changes, we will tell you before any charge, and you can leave first.

Ending

You can stop at any time and delete your projects. We may suspend an account that breaks these terms or puts the service at risk, and we will tell you why. If we close the service, we will give you notice and time to export your data.

Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, we will tell the owners of each organization by email before the change takes effect.
